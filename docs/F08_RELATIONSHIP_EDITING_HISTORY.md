# F-08 relationship editing, routing, and history closure

F-08 records one shared non-sequence relationship presentation contract: stable semantic and presentation endpoints, stable label and waypoint identities, explicit routing mode, transactional connector reconnection, two-level deletion, idempotent granular operations, and unambiguous containment presentation reuse. Geometry never determines semantic source or target.

The stage deliberately promotes no additional relationship type. DelegationConnector and all inspected relationship families remain partial because complete pointer/keyboard endpoint-handle, label, waypoint, containment-drag, import/reimport, and manual browser qualification is not yet available. Sequence Messages remain on the F-02 path.

Eight commits correspond to manifest, contract, endpoint handles, label/waypoint/routing, deletion, operation history, persistence/migration, and qualification. Node 22 and broad manual acceptance remain open. Generated browser artifacts are ignored and uncommitted.
